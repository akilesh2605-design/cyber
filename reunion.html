Reunion

Chapter 3

Two years, and a bag of books

You were preparing for JEE Main. I had my old JEE preparation books, and I decided to give them to you. They were useful, so they should be with someone who could use them. Some feelings are easier to express through actions.

It had been around two years since I had seen you. I was extremely nervous before coming to your home.

Then you made me feel comfortable. You smiled. We had fun. The nervousness slowly melted, and it felt easy.

About those books

I promise I never used JEE, or those books, to try to impress you or get anything out of it. I gave them to you the way a brother would, with no hidden intention. I know how much studying matters, because back when I needed it, I didn't have any IIT seniors, brothers or sisters, to guide me.

The chocolate

I had brought your favourite chocolate. And I was so nervous that I didn't give it to you. It stayed with me the whole time. So, sorry, chocolate. You deserved a better ending.

For me, seeing you again after those two years gave me a happiness I can hardly compare to anything else, even the happiness I felt when I got my IIT seat.

That is only how it felt for me. I'm not measuring it against anything. It was just a very happy day.
