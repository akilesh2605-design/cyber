The Years

Chapter 2

All these years

Life kept moving. Classes changed, schools changed, I grew up. But these feelings never completely went away. They just got quieter.

Class 9 · Class 7

The beginning. Your kindness. Your playfulness. I didn't know it would last this long.

The things I noticed

Your dancing. Your speeches, the confidence, the way people listened. Your English, your maths, your languages. Little things you said that probably meant nothing to you and stayed with me for years.

Our conversations

Random talks, the way you behaved, moments from school. I remember them from your Grade 7 days until now.

The quiet decision

At one point I heard from a friend that you weren't interested in relationships. So I chose not to force my feelings into your life. I simply kept them to myself. Your space mattered more than my wish to say it.

A new place

Even after reaching a place I had worked so hard to get into, and meeting many new people there, my feelings didn't simply disappear. That's only my own experience. It's just true for me.

Nothing here is a complaint. Some memories just stayed, and I wanted you to know they were there.
