What I Did

Chapter 4

The stupid things I did

This isn't a list of favours, and it isn't a bill. You don't owe me anything for any of it. These are simply things I did because you mattered to me.
  • Kept my feelings to myself instead of pushing them on you
  • Remembered small things about you
  • Came to meet you after around two years
  • Tried to learn Badugu, your language, back in Class 9 and 10. I don't really know why, even now
  • Brought your favourite chocolate (even if I froze)
  • Travelled back to Coimbatore hoping to see you
  • Kept caring, through distance and a busy college life
  • Missed two weeks of IIT orientation, because I postponed going just so I could see you in person first
  • Lied to you about a few small things, like saying I was at VIT, just to keep things simple
  • Spent more than ₹60,000 on this website and a personal server to host it. Probably too much, but it doesn't matter. It was meant to be something personal, just for you.

Coimbatore, the temple, Ooty

After a long time, I came back to my hometown, Coimbatore. I hoped I might see you at the temple festival.

I couldn't get a reserved train seat, so I travelled unreserved. It was a hard journey, but I kept thinking: maybe I'll see her there.

But you had gone to Ooty. So I didn't get to see you, and I felt very disappointed.

This story is not about blame. You did nothing wrong. You didn't know I was hoping to see you, and you had every right to be wherever you were. That day just showed me how much simply seeing you had come to mean to me.

Distance and busy days

I'm in my first year of college now, more than 500 km away, with projects, semester exams, internships, and lots of responsibilities. Life got busier and the distance got greater, but somehow you still remained one of the people with a special place in my thoughts.
